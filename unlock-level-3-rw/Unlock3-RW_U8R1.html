HOW SHOULD YOU INVEST YOUR MONEY?

1 Many people put their money in a bank because it is safe. However, the interest rate at most banks is quite low. As a result, some people choose to invest their money in stocks and shares instead because they hope their money will grow faster that way. Yet, investing in stocks can be risky. If the value of the stocks goes down, you could lose a lot of money. Many experts say you should invest only what you can afford to lose. So, what are some alternative investments for people who can afford to take big risks? Gold and classic cars are both popular investments.

10 year Gold Price USD/oz

2 Over time, gold has been a very good investment, though the price has fluctuated in recent years, as the graph shows. Between 2017 and 2020, for example, the price rose from $1,200 an ounce to a peak of $1,900 an ounce. Then, by the end of 2022, it had fallen to $1,800. By 2023, it rose again to $1,900. If you are thinking about investing in gold, you may want to consider one famous investor’s advice. Warren Buffett, one of the richest men in the world, dislikes gold as an investment. He points out that historically, the stock market has brought in significantly higher returns than gold. He also says it is better for society if you use your money for something productive, rather than have it sitting in a bank.

3 While gold and stocks are both investment options in the long term, some people prefer investments that they can use and enjoy now. For these people, classic cars are one way to have fun and make lots of money. In fact, over the last 30 or 40 years, the value of some classic cars has risen far more than that of gold or other investments like houses. As an example, a 1972 Ferrari Dino 246 GT cost around $13,000 in 1980 but was worth as much as $370,000 in 2023. A man in Tennessee bought the Aston Martin DB5 which was used in two of the James Bond films. This car cost just $7,000 in 1980 but sold in 2010 for an incredible $4.1 million! That is an incredible 20,000% increase! But neither of these cars compares to what is perhaps the best investment ever: A 1955 Mercedes-Benz 300SL cost about $36,000 in 1980 but is worth about $143 million now!

4 In sum, the prospect of making lots of money through investing is very exciting, but one must never forget that investing is a risky business. Gold prices rise, but they also fall. The classic car market also fluctuates and it is expensive to keep these cars in excellent condition. If you are clever and lucky, you may make a big profit – but remember, there are no guarantees.
